Why servers choose BBGames

Built for communities

BBGames originally began as a custom bot for Blueberryboom's server, so it is built with community at heart, not profits!

Fun without clutter

Games and fun commands like One Word Story, counting, and more, all in one bot!

Always evolving

New features are added every few weeks! Join the discord to suggest and also check out upcoming features.

Utility & Fun, together.

A fun, active server can be easily setup using just BBGames and built in discord automod (or a bot like Sapphire).

  • Fully customizable messages, from top to bottom.
  • Powering everything in your server from tickets to one word stories.
  • Simple and guided setup through slash commands!

Moderation without the chaos

Give your staff a break with an advanced ecosystem of connected modules and unique features!

  • Centralized logs for settings changes and other events
  • Advanced tickets system with workload tracking and transcripts
  • Unique features found in no other discord bots, like Variable Slowmode

Built-in games and useful commands

Keep your server active with lightweight games and repeatable engagement systems that bring people back every day.

  • TicTacToe, dice, dad jokes, and coin flips
  • Levelling, achievements, counting, and one-word story games
  • All commands are designed to be raid-protected, like /afk which deletes messages after 5 seconds!
